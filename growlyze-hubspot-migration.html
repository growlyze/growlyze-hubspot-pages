      FAQ

      Questions teams ask before they move.

      30 minutes with a solution architect, not a salesperson.

      Will we lose any data or automations?

      No. We back up everything, test records and automations first, and you sign off a written reconciliation report before your old CRM is switched off. If anything is ever missing, we fix it free.

      What happens to our custom objects?

      They’re rebuilt as HubSpot custom objects on Enterprise, with every field and association mapped and validated.

      What about Apex triggers and Flows?

      Our experts rebuild the logic as HubSpot workflows and custom-coded actions, redesigned for HubSpot’s data model and tested before go-live.

      Do attachments and email history come across?

      Yes, always: emails, calls, tasks, meetings, notes and files, with original dates and owners.

      Do our email campaigns and nurture sequences come across?

      Yes. Lists, templates, journeys, drips and sequences are rebuilt in HubSpot, with subscription and consent preferences kept.

      What about call logging and our dialer?

      Call history moves with each record, and your dialer is connected to HubSpot (or replaced by HubSpot calling), with outcomes and recordings logged automatically.

      Will internal alerts and notifications still work?

      Yes. Slack, Teams and email alerts, owner assignment, task creation and SLA notifications are rebuilt and tested before go-live.

      Will contacts get duplicate emails during the switch?

      No. Old automations are paused before the new ones are switched on, so nobody gets a double email or alert.

      Will our team have downtime?

      We cut over on a weekend, so your team logs out of the old CRM on Friday and into HubSpot on Monday.

      Can we keep Salesforce for sales?

      Yes. Many clients keep Salesforce for qualified sales data and use HubSpot for marketing and their full lead database. Hybrid setup is from $2,000, and free with any migration.

      Our renewal is soon. Can you hit our date?

      Tell us your deadline on your free plan call. Your go-live date is guaranteed in writing.

      What about CPQ and quotes?

      Products, price books and quotes move into HubSpot, and quotes, invoices and payments can run in HubSpot while your accounting software stays connected.

      Which platforms do you migrate from?

      Salesforce, Pipedrive, Zoho (CRM, Campaigns and Desk), monday.com, Microsoft Dynamics, Close, Insightly, Keap, GoHighLevel, Airtable and spreadsheets, plus Pardot, Marketo, Mailchimp, ActiveCampaign, Klaviyo and Constant Contact, and Zendesk, Freshdesk, Intercom, Gorgias and Zoho Desk.

      Do you merge HubSpot portals?

      Yes. We merge two HubSpot portals into one after an acquisition, with the same reconciliation process.

      Do we still pay HubSpot’s onboarding fee?

      When we onboard you as part of the migration, HubSpot’s onboarding fee can be waived, subject to eligibility.

      Is HubSpot setup included?

      Yes. Your Hubs are set up, with 3 live training sessions and 30 days of support.

      How do you keep our data secure?

      Encrypted transfers, no local copies, access removed at the end, and for sensitive data we work entirely inside your own cloud.
